How the bank is used: the model first uses it against this ship's GHG-intensity deficit, then carries any unused balance forward. For example, a 100 tCO₂e bank covering a 60 tCO₂e deficit leaves 40 tCO₂e. This model does not offer banked surplus through a pool.

How penalty history is used: if the first case year also incurs a deficit penalty, 1 preceding penalty year gives a 10% uplift; 2 gives 20%. These are additions to the standard penalty, not compounded increases. A year without a modelled deficit penalty resets the streak. Historical penalty payments are not charged again.
