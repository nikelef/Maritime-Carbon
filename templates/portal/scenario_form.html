Possible 2% renewable e-fuel requirement from 2034

What this means: From 2034, ships may need to get at least 2% of their fuel energy covered by FuelEU from qualifying renewable e-fuels, such as renewable hydrogen or e-methanol. These are called RFNBOs (renewable fuels of non-biological origin).

Whether this requirement applies depends on EU-wide uptake in 2031 and the European Commission's assessment of fuel availability and prices. FuelEU Article 5 ↗

If you are unsure, keep the default: the calculator shows how far each ship falls below 2%, without adding a penalty for this possible requirement. This section has no effect on years before 2034.

What the estimate covers

The 2% is measured by fuel energy, excluding shore electricity. This estimate does not apply RFNBO-specific pooling, banking or exemptions for other fuels with equivalent emissions savings.
