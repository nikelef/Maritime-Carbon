EU berth is separate from sea voyages. In the ordinary scope used here, intra-EU sea and EU berth count at 100%; EU ↔ non-EU sea at 50%. Other sea and non-EU berth fuel count toward purchase costs, with 0% FuelEU/ETS coverage.

For EU berth rows, enter the share of fuel consumption that shore electricity can replace, or leave blank to use the shore-power default below. Use 0% for boilers and other consumption that cannot be replaced. This percentage has no effect on other categories.

For a proposed wind retrofit, complete both wind columns on every sea row, including explicit zeros. See Wings & wind-assisted propulsion below for the baseline and net-savings rules. Imports start without wind settings; review them here.

HSFO is available for sea and berth use. Confirm the applicable sulphur limit, an approved scrubber or other compliance method where needed, and local operating/discharge restrictions. Selecting a fuel does not establish permission to use it.

MGO/LSMGO uses the MGO entry in the fuel library. Existing rows without voyage numbers start under Voyage 1; assign them to the appropriate voyages. Older sea percentages and separate MGO pilot/berth quantities are converted into rows.

Fuel switching boundaries

Evaluate one alternative system per vessel in this case. LNG needs an engine selection; renewable fuels also need eligibility evidence in the fuel library. The optimizer chooses which current sea fuels to reduce, including current pilot fuel, within the overall ship limit. Replacement is selected separately for each voyage and sea leg, with alternative energy supplied on the same leg. FuelEU separately prioritizes the eligible annual energy inventory by WtW intensity; ETS retains each leg’s proportional scope. Berth fuel changes only when shore power is selected. A replacement share is not permission to blend incompatible fuels in one tank.
