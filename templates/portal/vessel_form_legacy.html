Fuel switching boundaries

Evaluate one alternative system per vessel in this case. LNG needs an engine selection; renewable fuels also need eligibility evidence in the fuel library. The optimizer chooses which current sea fuels to reduce, including current pilot fuel, within the overall ship limit. Replacement is selected separately for each voyage and sea leg, with alternative energy supplied on the same leg. FuelEU separately prioritizes the eligible annual energy inventory by WtW intensity; ETS retains each leg’s proportional scope. A replacement share is not permission to blend incompatible fuels in one tank.
