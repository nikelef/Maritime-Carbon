Bring your fleet into the case.

Import vessel profiles and the same voyage, category and fuel rows used by the editor.

Download Excel template ↓CSV alternative ↓

Fill the workbook in two steps

  1. Vessels: one row per ship, with a unique vessel_id, identity, nominated alternative fuel and maximum alternative share.
  2. Consumption: one row per vessel, voyage, sea/berth category and current fuel. Repeat the vessel_id and enter full annual tonnes.

Keep the sheet names and row-1 headers. The workbook includes Instructions, drop-down choices and a separate Example sheet. Only Vessels and Consumption are imported.

Enter percentages as numbers from 0 to 100. MGO represents MGO / LSMGO. Do not halve EU/non-EU tonnes; coverage is applied by the calculator. Leave shore-power share blank or zero when no shore power is planned.

Import adds ships; it does not update or replace existing vessels. Uploading the same workbook again adds them again. The whole upload must pass validation before any vessel is saved.
